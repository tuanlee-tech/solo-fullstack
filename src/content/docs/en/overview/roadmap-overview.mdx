## 1. The 3-Step Journey

<Steps>
1. **Learn the Common Core — 3 mandatory blocks**

   Frontend, Backend, and DevOps basics. Everything both career paths require 100%.

2. **Stop at the Branching Point**

   Assess your real situation: finances, product ideas, market opportunities, personal energy.

3. **Choose Branch A or Branch B**

   Go corporate (Branch A) or go independent (Branch B). You can switch branches later if circumstances change.
</Steps>

## 3. The Branching Point — Decision Criteria

Don't decide in advance based on gut feeling. When you reach the checkpoint (~month 9–12), evaluate:

<table class="branch-table">
  <thead>
    <tr>
      <th>Criteria</th>
      <th>⬡ Branch A — Staff/Architect</th>
      <th>⬡ Branch B — Solo Founder</th>
    </tr>
  </thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <td><strong>Finances</strong></td>
      <td>No 6–12 month runway saved</td>
      <td>Have savings to live 6–12 months without income</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td><strong>Product</strong></td>
      <td>No validated product idea</td>
      <td>Have an idea with real user signals</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td><strong>Job Market</strong></td>
      <td>Received a good Senior/Fullstack offer</td>
      <td>Market is tough, or offers aren't compelling</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td><strong>Personal</strong></td>
      <td>Need stable income for family</td>
      <td>Few constraints, accept income volatility</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td><strong>Energy</strong></td>
      <td>Somewhat exhausted, need a stable pace</td>
      <td>Still highly motivated to be fully independent</td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

> This is not a permanent, one-way decision. You can take Branch A for a few years to build capital/experience, then switch to B — or vice versa.
